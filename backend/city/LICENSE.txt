The source snapshot is included, with per-record OSM links and source IDs. The normalized dataset, CSV and metadata are openly available in this package.

Mapping coverage is incomplete and may contain duplicate/mis-tagged/closed facilities. The bounding box includes surrounding municipalities. An OSM hospital tag does not certify clinical services or available beds.

Street data: OpenStreetMap Overpass snapshot, 2026-10-08T12:57:06Z.
The bundled roads_snapshot.json, street_graph.json and chennai_streets.json are map database derivatives under ODbL 1.0. Street graph coverage is selected motor-driving corridor classes, not every street. Raw records retain OSM way IDs, geometry and source tags; rebuild scripts are included.
